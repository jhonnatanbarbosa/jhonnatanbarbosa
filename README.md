## Now — Dead Saints Parade
 
A third-person stealth immersive sim built solo in **Unity 6.3** and self-published under Jhorro Productions. You play a fragile spy infiltrating a Gothic-Victorian world where information is the real weapon and direct combat is a death sentence. Tension is engineered through interacting systems rather than scripting: patrol AI, two-zone vision cones with a suspicion model, light/shadow detection, sound propagation, cover, lockpicking, and a journal/clue system, all built from scratch. Inspired by Thief, Dishonored, Metal Gear Solid, and Gloomwood.
 
`Solo dev & self-published` · `PC / Steam Deck` · `17 systems live` · `Steam Next Fest demo Feb 2027` · `Full release Q2 2027`
